Foundations of Faith

Understanding the principles of the Gospel.

The Nature of Sin

Sin is any act, feeling, or thought that goes against God's perfect law and character. It separates us from God, but the good news is that Jesus Christ paid the price for our sins on the cross.

Romans 3:23

"...for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God."

Romans 6:23

"For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."

1 John 1:9

"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness."

The Ordinance of Baptism

Baptism is an outward expression of an inward commitment to Jesus Christ. It symbolizes the death of our old, sinful selves and our resurrection to a new life in Him. It is a public declaration of our faith.

Matthew 28:19

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."

Acts 2:38

"Peter replied, 'Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.'"

Romans 6:4

"We were therefore buried with him through baptism into death in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory of the Father, we too may live a new life."

The Gift of the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is the third person of the Trinity, given to believers as a comforter, guide, and source of power. He dwells within us, seals our salvation, and empowers us to live a godly life and be effective witnesses for Christ.

John 14:26

"But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things and will remind you of everything I have said to you."

Acts 1:8

"But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."

Galatians 5:22-23

"But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control. Against such things there is no law."

The Call to Repentance

Repentance is a sincere turning away from sin and turning toward God. It involves a change of mind, heart, and direction. It is not just feeling sorry for sin, but a conscious decision to forsake it and follow Christ.

Acts 3:19

"Repent, then, and turn to God, so that your sins may be wiped out, that times of refreshing may come from the Lord."

2 Corinthians 7:10

"Godly sorrow brings repentance that leads to salvation and leaves no regret, but worldly sorrow brings death."

Luke 13:3

"I tell you, no! But unless you repent, you too will all perish."
